# QA Barriers

Every required defect is a barrier. There is no “non-barrier” defect class.

## Mathematics
- every question independently solved;
- answer and working correct;
- no unintended ambiguity;
- correct terminology, units and notation;
- diagrams and labels mathematically valid;
- intended skill actually assessed.

## Assessment design
- intended progression is present;
- marks match observable evidence;
- unnecessary repetition is absent;
- Q7 is genuinely complex B-level evidence where that is the target;
- Q8 is genuinely complex A-level evidence where that is the target;
- Q7 and Q8 are independent;
- context is meaningful.

## Student usability
- no unnecessary instructions;
- no redundant boxes, labels or tables;
- adequate working space;
- readable at print scale;
- no accidental scaffolding that removes the intended reasoning.

## Presentation
- A4 portrait where required;
- consistent hierarchy;
- no clipping, overflow, collisions or off-page content;
- diagrams aligned and readable;
- no accidental answer material.

## Marking key
- exact duplicate of the final student assessment;
- same numbers, diagrams, wording and marks;
- correct answers;
- working where required;
- acceptable alternatives identified where appropriate.

## Curriculum rationale
- based on the final assessment;
- curriculum claims evidenced by actual questions;
- no unsupported claims.

## Verdict
Any failed item means `NOT READY`.
