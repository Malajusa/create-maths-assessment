# Mathematics Conventions

Apply mathematically conventional notation appropriate to the assessed year level.

## General

- Use Australian English.
- Use SI units and correct unit symbols.
- Put a space between number and unit where conventional, e.g. `25 cm`.
- Use the multiplication sign `×` in student-facing arithmetic rather than a programming asterisk.
- Use `÷` or fraction notation where appropriate to the year level.
- Keep fraction notation legible and unambiguous.
- Do not introduce unnecessary decimal precision or recurring decimals where they are not part of the construct.

## Geometry

- Use capital letters for vertex labels where conventional.
- Triangle names must correspond to their vertex labels.
- Angle labels must unambiguously identify the intended angle.
- Do not place labels so they imply an unstated property.
- If a whole shape is identified as shape A/B/etc., place that identifier centrally inside the shape unless doing so interferes with solving the problem.
- Do not confuse a shape identifier with a vertex label.
- Maintain conventional right-angle, equal-side, parallel and other mathematical markings.

## Visual integrity

A diagram is mathematical information. Any redraw, resize or number change must preserve the intended relationships and must be revalidated mathematically.
