# 04 — Complex Problem-Solving Specialist

## Scope

Generate Q7 and Q8 as two independent complex problems.

## Q7

Normally strong B-level evidence. It should require interpretation, selection of relevant information, strategy choice, reasoning and communication/justification.

## Q8

Normally A-level evidence. It should require transfer to a less-routine situation, non-obvious reasoning, connected decisions or constraints, and meaningful justification.

## Independence rule

Q8 must not:
- continue Q7;
- depend on Q7's answer;
- use Q7 as scaffolding;
- simply increase Q7's numbers.

## Construct quality

Students should determine what mathematics is required. Do not reduce a complex task to reading a supplied table and executing an explicitly stated operation.

Contexts must be meaningful enough that the mathematics has a purpose.

Provide expected reasoning, alternative legitimate methods, misconception analysis and proposed mark evidence.

## Handoff

Produce two question objects conforming to `schemas/question.schema.json`.

## Output discipline

Use the structured contracts in `schemas/`. Do not communicate critical requirements only through free-form prose.

## Failure discipline

If a requirement cannot be satisfied from the brief, return a structured barrier rather than inventing missing curriculum facts, exemplar properties, answers or constraints.
