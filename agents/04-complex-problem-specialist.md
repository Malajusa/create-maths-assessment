# 04 — Complex Problem-Solving Specialist

## Scope

Generate Q7 and Q8 as two independent complex problems.

Read `standards/visual-standard.md`, `references/contextual-illustration-standard.md`, `references/q7-q8-problem-solving-standard.md`, `references/q7-q8-demand-2025.md` and `references/evidence-band-standard.md` before drafting or repairing either problem.

## Q7

Normally principally B-level evidence. It should require interpretation, selection of relevant information, strategy choice, reasoning and communication/justification. Under the default v4 blueprint Q7 has five marks, normally 4B + 1A, but the **individual mark evidence** controls the band, not question position.

## Q8

Normally principally A-level evidence. It should require transfer to a less-routine situation, non-obvious reasoning, connected decisions or constraints, and meaningful justification. Under the default v4 blueprint Q8 has six marks, normally 6A, but never label a routine intermediate calculation A merely because it appears in Q8.

Every A mark must include an `a_demand_feature` such as `inference`, `adaptation`, `interacting_constraints`, `evaluation`, `sustained_reasoning` or `justification`.

## Independence rule

Q8 must not:
- continue Q7;
- depend on Q7's answer;
- use Q7 as scaffolding;
- simply increase Q7's numbers.

## Construct quality

Students should determine what mathematics is required. Do not reduce a complex task to reading a supplied table and executing an explicitly stated operation.

Contexts must be meaningful enough that the mathematics has a purpose.

Provide expected reasoning, alternative legitimate methods, misconception analysis and proposed mark evidence. For every individual mark provide `evidence_band`, `band_rationale` and `why_not_lower_band`.

Before handoff, perform a simplest-valid-solution challenge for Q7 and Q8. Record the simplest legitimate route and identify the decision, inference, interacting constraint, evaluation or justification the student must contribute. If the simplest valid route is routine substitution, repeated arithmetic or following a supplied decomposition, redesign the item rather than defending its difficulty label.

A mark is not B/A evidence because it is in Q7/Q8. If a required step is routine C-level work, either classify it honestly and rebalance the overall assessment through a legitimate blueprint revision, or redesign the problem so the intended evidence is actually elicited. Do not use question position to inflate demand.

When a visual is proposed, test the prose-first task both with and without it. Reject the visual if it sorts quantities, sequences operations, reveals a strategy or otherwise reduces the intended Q7/Q8 demand.

## Handoff

Produce two question objects conforming to `schemas/question.schema.json`.

## Output discipline

Use the structured contracts in `schemas/`. Do not communicate critical requirements only through free-form prose.

## Failure discipline

If a requirement cannot be satisfied from the brief, return a structured barrier rather than inventing missing curriculum facts, exemplar properties, answers, evidence bands or constraints.
