# Visual proof artefacts

These original, distributable SVGs demonstrate the two modes defined by `standards/visual-standard.md`.

- `canonical-figures-proof.svg` shows the approved square, circle and equilateral triangle treatment at normal and compact assessment sizes, with restrained mathematical annotation.
- `problem-page-proof.svg` demonstrates an original contextual problem composition: concise prose, a simple exact diagram, generous working space and an additive greyscale-safe marking overlay.

They are calibration proofs, not student-facing templates and not copies of the supplied NAPLAN, dictionary or problem-card artwork. Mathematical geometry should be regenerated from the registered assets or constructors rather than traced from these sheets.
